# Develop the research output

Use a simple outline and adapt it to the audience:

1. Question and why the answer could be useful.
2. Closest work and the contribution this project investigates.
3. Evidence, data, methods, and checks.
4. Findings and the conditions under which they hold.
5. Limitations, uncertainty, and unanswered questions.
6. References, contribution credit, and material agent assistance.

Link each material claim to a source or result. Keep editable text and figure sources. Ask for human review before publication.

A substantive blog, preprint, full conference paper, or journal article can be an output. State its peer-review status. Record public outputs in the [OSRI register](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/osri/blob/main/publications/README.md).

Follow [Publish and record your research](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/guides/publications/publish/) for destination choice, file preparation, human authorization, submission, revisions, preservation, and registration. Ask a maintainer for help with the package or archive. A forum announcement is a link to the output, not a substitute for its research record.
